# MatchReplay

A sports replay platform which provides links of the match replays for the users without letting them know the result to give them a live-like watching experience.

Features:

1. Homepage shows all FIFA World Cup 2026 matches.
Format: 
World Cup Match

Argentina 🇦🇷 vs France 🇫🇷
Match Time
Watch Replay (link 1 link 2 ...)

Brazil 🇧🇷 vs Germany 🇩🇪
Match Time
Watch Replay (link 1 link 2 ...)
2. No scores.
3. No spoiler thumbnails.
4. Click the link to watch replay.
5. Bright/Dark theme (adjustable).
6. Future support for replay discovery AI Agent.
